## What we collect

**When you log in with Steam**
- Your SteamID, Steam display name and avatar. Steam tells us these when you log in; we don't receive your Steam password or email address.
- When you joined this site and when you were last active on it.

**What you add to the site**
- Your profile bio.
- Forum posts, threads, likes and dislikes, and the reputation they earn you.
- Support tickets and their replies.
- Files and images you upload.

**When you play on our servers**
Our servers report, for each player (whether or not they have an account here):
- SteamID and in-game name.
- When you first joined and last played on each server, and your total playtime.
- Your in-game rank (usergroup).
- Gamemode statistics, such as in-game money or job.

**When you buy something**
- What you bought, the price, discounts applied, and whether it was delivered in-game or refunded.
- Payments are handled by Stripe. Your card details go directly to Stripe and never reach us. Stripe's privacy policy is at https://stripe.com/privacy.

**Moderation**
- Bans and suspensions, with the reason, who issued them and when.

**Notifications**
- The notifications we show you on the site. Read notifications are deleted after 30 days.

## Cookies

We use two cookies, both needed for the site to work:
- `garrynet_token` keeps you logged in. It expires after 24 hours or when you log out.
- `garrynet_login_state` protects the Steam login against forgery. It lasts a few minutes.

We don't use advertising or tracking cookies. [Remove this line, or list them here, if you add analytics.]

## Why we use it

- To log you in and show your profile.
- To run the forums, support and store.
- To deliver purchases to your Steam account in-game.
- To moderate the community and keep banned players off our servers.
- To show statistics such as playtime on profiles and server pages.

## Who can see it

- Your profile, forum posts and playtime statistics are public.
- Support tickets are visible to you and our support team.
- Purchases are visible to you and staff who manage the store.
- We share payment information with Stripe to process payments.
- Our hosting provider ([Hosting Provider]) stores the data on our behalf.

We don't sell your data.

## How long we keep it

We keep your account and the content you post until you ask us to delete it. Player records are kept while the servers they belong to exist. Purchase records are kept for as long as we need them for accounting and legal reasons.
